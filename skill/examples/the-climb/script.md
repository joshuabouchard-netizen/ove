# "The Climb" (action cut, about 45 s, 16:9)

**The idea:** a first version was a calm lecture over diagrams and came out boring. This one is a ride: we follow **one drop of
water** from the watering can, down into the soil, up the plant like a rocket, and out into the
sky. First-person (FPV drone-style) flights through soil, root and stem; slow-motion impacts;
fast cuts (about 17 shots, ~2.5 s each instead of ~5 s); an energetic narrator; a driving
percussive score; and sound design (whooshes, impacts, a riser into the snap, a sub-drop).
On-screen words slam in for the big beats.

One fact worth checking twice: the tallest tree, Hyperion, is about
116 m (380 ft), so its water column is taller than a 30-story building.

## Narration (≈111 words, delivered fast and excited, ~165 wpm)

```
This drop of water is about to climb your plant, with no heart and no pump. It squeezes into a root, then shoots up a tube thinner than a hair. Up top, water escapes through tiny pores in the leaves, and because water molecules grip each other, every one that leaves yanks the whole chain up behind it. In the tallest redwoods, that chain is taller than a thirty-story building. Pull too hard, and it snaps, with a pop too high for you to hear. Most of the water blasts out into the sky. But sunlight rips a little of it apart, and the oxygen it frees? You're breathing it right now.
```

## Sound

- **Music:** Lyria, ~45–60 s, driving cinematic hybrid percussion, pulsing synth bass, ~125 bpm,
  with a drop at the start, a cut-out at the snap and a big final hit. Instrumental.
- **SFX (made locally with ffmpeg, free):** impact for the splash, whooshes on the FPV moves,
  thuds under NO HEART / NO PUMP, a riser into the snap, a crack + sub-drop on the snap.
- **Voice:** Gemini TTS, energetic and excited, a touch of trailer-narrator drama. Audition
  Puck / Fenrir / Charon on line 1.
